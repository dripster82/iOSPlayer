Support

Email barley_gilt6r@icloud.com, or open an issue at github.com/dripster82/iOSPlayer/issues. If something failed to play, Settings → About → Diagnostics has a log you can share; it contains no passwords.

Getting started

  1. Open Reel and tap Get started to add a NAS share or a folder, or Try with sample media to look around first.
  2. For titles and artwork, add a free TMDB API key in Settings → Metadata (create an account at themoviedb.org, then Settings → API).
  3. Reel scans your source in the background and sorts what it finds into TV Shows and Films.

Common questions

A title is matched to the wrong thing

Long-press it, or open its menu, and choose Fix match. For anime, use the Anime option in that sheet.

Some files are in Needs Review

Reel was not sure what they are. Open Needs Review from Home and accept, fix or ignore them, one file or a whole folder at a time.

Playback stops or spins

Reel reconnects on its own when a share drops. If it gives up, the error card has Retry and a Copy details button for the log. Check the NAS is awake; Reel can send Wake-on-LAN if you enter the NAS's MAC address in the source settings.

Another app offers to open a video in Reel

Reel accepts files from Files, share sheets and reel:// links. Links in VLC's own format can be turned on in Settings → Library.

Data providers

  • TMDB. Titles, artwork, episode names, cast and collections. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • fanart.tv. Optional logos and extra backgrounds, with your own project key.
  • TheTVDB. Optional fallback search for shows, with your own key and subscriber PIN. Metadata provided by TheTVDB. Please consider adding missing information or subscribing.
  • OpenSubtitles. Optional subtitle search and download, with your own key and account.
  • AniDB and Anime-Lists. Anime title matching, using AniDB's public titles list and the Anime-Lists mapping to TMDB.
  • VLCKit by VideoLAN, LGPL 2.1, for playback.
